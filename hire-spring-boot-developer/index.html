Why hire a freelancer instead of an agency?

You work directly with the developer writing your code. There are no account managers or handoffs between teams, decisions are made quickly, and you avoid agency overhead.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short description of your product, who uses it, what already exists and any deadlines. Designs, API docs or repository access help, but they are not needed for the first call.

Can you join my existing team?

Yes. I can work inside your repository, follow your code style and review process, and join standups or planning calls when that is useful.

Do you also build the frontend?

My focus is the backend. I build clean, documented APIs that your web or mobile developer can plug into, and I work closely with them so the integration goes smoothly.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. I am happy to sign an NDA before you share the details of your product.

How do payments work?

You pay per milestone, as agreed in the written quote. Each milestone ends with working software you can review, so you always see what you are paying for.
